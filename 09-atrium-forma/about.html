About the studio

Eleven people, two offices, three commissions a year.

Founded 2014

Lukas Verhoeven and Hana Mori met as second-year students at TU Delft, in 2006. They spent five years afterwards working together at SANAA's Tokyo office — Lukas on the Toledo Museum, Hana on the Louvre-Lens. They founded Atrium Forma in Antwerp in March 2014. The Kyoto office opened in 2018.

The studio works almost exclusively on cultural and civic buildings — libraries, chapels, museums, schools. We accept three new commissions per year, regardless of fee. Closing the studio between commissions four and eleven is the only way we have found to do this kind of work properly.

The studio is eleven people: Lukas and Hana, four senior architects, three junior, two interns, and Marieke, the office manager who has been there since the first day. Half of the studio is in Antwerp, half in Kyoto. We rotate quarterly. We talk every morning at 9 Antwerp time / 5pm Kyoto time, the only hour when both offices are awake.

We have completed fourteen buildings since founding — five libraries, three museums, four chapels, two schools. Three more are under construction. We were finalists for the Mies van der Rohe Award in 2024 for the Kortrijk Public Library, and won the Belgian Building Award in 2022.

We are not interested in scaling. We are not opening more offices. We are not taking on private residential work, hospitality, or commercial. The brief is the building, and the building must be honest about its function. A library should disappear behind the books. A chapel should disappear behind the light.

— Lukas Verhoeven & Hana Mori · Antwerp / Kyoto · 2024

Principals

Two architects.

Lukas Verhoeven
Founding principal · based Antwerp

TU Delft 2008, MArch. Five years at SANAA Tokyo on the Toledo Museum and the Louvre-Lens. Co-founded Atrium Forma 2014. Visiting critic at TU Delft and ETH Zürich. Born in Hasselt, Belgium.

Hana Mori
Founding principal · based Kyoto

TU Delft 2008, MArch. Five years at SANAA Tokyo on the Louvre-Lens and Grace Farms. Co-founded Atrium Forma 2014. Adjunct professor, Kyoto Institute of Technology. Born in Kyoto.
