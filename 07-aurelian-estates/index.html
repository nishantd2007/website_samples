The brokerage

Founded by an editor, not a salesperson.

Margaux Whitfield ran the architecture desk at Dwell for nine years before founding Aurelian in 2018. The thesis was simple: a great house is a long story, and most brokerages tell it badly.

Aurelian represents eight to twelve houses a year — capped on purpose. Every property is written up in a 96-page printed brief. Half of our 4% fee is donated to the local historical society of the property.
