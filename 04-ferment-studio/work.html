Selected case studies · 2023 – 2026

Eleven brands. Zero beige.

A short reel of the work we’ve put into the world recently. We share full case studies on the call, not the website — but here’s the shape of it.

Brand identity Packaging Launch 2026 · Oaxaca, MX

Brawler & Sons.

A small-batch mezcal from Oaxaca founded by three siblings who don’t agree on anything except the agave. We named the brand, designed the bottle, and ran the launch in four U.S. markets. The label is handprinted on each bottle by the eldest brother. He insisted.

Sold out in 14 days First production run of 6,000 bottles. Now a 9-month waitlist.
Naming Brand identity UX writing 2026 · Seattle

Pebble Hardware.

A boutique smart-home operating system competing in a category drowning in robot-themed names. We named it Pebble, built an identity that feels closer to Muji than to Sonos, and wrote every line of voice — from setup screens to the alarm chime label.

Brand Packaging E-comm 2025 · Brooklyn

Ghostfruit Soda.

A non-alcoholic apéritif from a former bartender who got sober at 31 and realized everything in the NA aisle tasted like an apology. We built the brand, designed the cans, wrote the manifesto on the box, and shipped the e-comm site. The brand voice is allowed to swear.

IdentityPackagingVoiceWebPhotography
Whole Foods national rollout, Q3 From kitchen counter to 488 stores in 14 months.
Identity Strategy Editorial 2025 · London

Forager Bank.

A challenger bank for self-employed people — the freelancers, the moonlighters, the chefs running pop-ups out of garages. The category was full of friendly purple. We built a brand that looks like a magazine, sounds like a friend, and shows actual numbers on the home page.

180k signups in the first 60 days 5× their internal forecast. We were also surprised.
