Manifesto

We started Ferment
because we were
bored.

The short version.

Founded 2021. Two founders. Six humans now. Profitable from year one.

In the summer of 2021, the two of us quit holding-company agencies after pitching the same beer brand the same way three years in a row. Our seventh round of "youthful, premium, approachable." We walked. We rented a feed-store warehouse in East Cesar Chavez with terrible AC and a kombucha brewer in the corner. We named the place after that brewer, sort of.

Ferment is the studio we wanted to work at, but had to build instead. The pitch: independent, opinionated, allergic to "stakeholder feedback," small on purpose. Six people, no offshore deck factory, no SDRs, and a hard cap of four active clients at a time.

The reason most brands taste the same is that most agencies cook from the same pot. We don't. The studio is profitable, we don't take work that isn't ours to do, and we are very, deliberately, slow to grow.

— Dee & Bruno, August 2021

Six principles

Things we don't pretend not to believe.

01

Brands aren't logos.

They're the cumulative impression of every touchpoint. We design the impression first, the logo last.

02

The brief is the answer.

The two weeks we spend on the brief are the two weeks that matter most. We won't skip them.

03

Small on purpose.

Six people. Four active clients. Capped. The day we go above this, we are no longer good at it.

04

Founders touch everything.

Dee or Bruno are on every deliverable. No "creative supervision" from a beach in Tulum.

05

We write our own words.

We don't outsource copy. The voice of a brand is too important to ship to a freelancer pool.

06

The work is the pitch.

We don't do spec work, slides about ourselves, or three-hour creds decks. Our case studies pitch us.

Brief us if you've made it this far.

If the manifesto resonates, the brief will too. If not — no hard feelings, you can stop reading.
