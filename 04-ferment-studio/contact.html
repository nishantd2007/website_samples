How this works.

Send the brief. We read every one — usually within 48 hours. If there's a fit, we book a 30-minute call. If not, we tell you why and refer you to someone we'd send our friends to.

What we want to know.

Who you are. What you're making. Why now. Budget if you have a number. Timeline if you have one. The rougher the better.

What we don't.

NDAs before a first conversation. Spec work. A six-stakeholder kickoff. A "fit assessment quiz." Three reference checks before a call.
