01 / Powertrain

Dual motor. Eight-hundred volts.

Permanent-magnet synchronous motors at both axles, sourced from a private partnership with a Stuttgart-based Tier-1 supplier. Torque-vectoring, single-speed reduction gearbox. Liquid-cooled, hand-soldered busbars.

02 / Chassis

A monocoque, laid up by hand.

The Volton One's chassis is a single-piece carbon monocoque, laid up by Henrik Mayer in our Stuttgart workshop. Same engineer who built the 2002 Carrera GT prototype tub. One chassis takes six weeks.

03 / Interior

Two seats. Plus two, if you ask.

The cabin is a 2+2, finished in Tuscan tan saddle leather as standard. Wool boucle, Loro Piana cashmere, and bare carbon-twill available. Every cabin is fitted by Anna Schmidt — twenty-six years at Mercedes Maybach.

04 / The Voice

The cabin sounds.
The street does not.

The Voice is a closed-circuit acoustic system. The output is conducted through the chassis directly to the driver. It is silent to a microphone placed three feet from the car.
