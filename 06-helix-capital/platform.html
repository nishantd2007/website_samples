02 · Tax intelligence

Loss harvesting at the lot level.

Our tax engine looks at every lot in every account in your household. It harvests losses when it sees them, avoids wash sales across spouses' accounts, and projects estimated taxes in real time.

  • Automatic loss harvesting at the lot level
  • Cross-account wash-sale avoidance
  • RSU sell-on-vest rules customizable by ticker
  • 83(b) and ISO/NSO modeling included
  • October tax-projection meeting with a CPA, every year

RSUs, ISOs, NSOs — modeled.

  • 83(b) decision modeling, before you file
  • ISO/NSO exercise modeling (AMT included)
  • Concentration risk scoring across employer ticker
  • Pre-IPO liquidity planning
