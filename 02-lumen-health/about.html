About Lumen

We are building the primary care office we wish we had.

Lumen began in a kitchen in Bernal Heights, with one physician and one product person frustrated by exactly the same thing — that medicine had become reactive, transactional, and short.

Dr. Maya Iyer, MD
Co-founder · Medical Director

Internal medicine, UCSF. Eight years in primary care, four in cardiology research. The doctor everyone in her hospital called when their own families got sick. Believes you can read more in fifteen unhurried minutes than in fifteen rushed visits.

Sarah Chen
Co-founder · CEO

Former product lead on Apple Health. Watched her mother get a stage-three diagnosis that should have been caught at stage one. Quit her job the following week. Spent the year researching every preventive care model in the country before calling Maya.

Our principles

Six things we keep on the wall.

01

Time is the medicine

Average primary care visit: 7 minutes. Ours: 45. Most diagnoses live in the second half of the conversation.

02

One physician, one patient

You see the same doctor every time. Continuity is the most underrated drug in the pharmacy.

03

Data we both can read

Your labs, your trends, your plan — in your portal, written in plain English. No PDF buried in a patient portal you forgot existed.

04

Prevention is a verb

We measure quarterly. We move the needle quarterly. Wellness isn't a once-a-year photograph.

05

Small panel

Each physician serves no more than 400 patients. We turn members away rather than spread thin.

06

You text us back

Real human messages. Same-day response by 5pm, every weekday. Yes, even on Mondays.

“The mission isn't fancy: return primary care to what it was meant to be — a long relationship between two people who actually know each other.”

Dr. Maya Iyer
Co-founder, Medical Director
