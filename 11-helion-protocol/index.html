The problem

Three clouds own the world's GPUs.
Five hundred thousand more sit idle in homes.

AWS, Azure, and GCP control 67% of available AI compute and have raised prices 4× since 2022. Meanwhile half a million GPU workstations sit at < 10% utilization in research labs, render farms, and homes. Helion is the protocol that meets the two ends.

Verifiable compute

Every job is settled with a zk-proof of execution. Operators can't cheat. Workloads can't be tampered with. The proof is on-chain in under 200 ms.

Operator margins, not platform margins

70% of every job's revenue settles to the GPU operator in USDC. 25% goes to compute orchestrators. The protocol takes 5%. Compare to AWS at ~62% margin.

One SDK, every framework

PyTorch, JAX, vLLM, TensorFlow, MLX — Helion supports the existing stack. Drop in one runtime adapter, point your training script at the network.

How it works

Four steps from training script to settlement.

01

Submit

Your training job is submitted via the SDK. The protocol shards it, fingerprints it, and posts the requirements (GPU class, FP16, RAM) on chain.

02

Match

Orchestrators bid for the job. The protocol matches it to operators based on reputation, latency, price, and locality.

03

Execute & prove

The operator executes the workload inside a TEE. A zk-proof of correct execution is generated alongside the result.

04

Settle

Proof verifies on-chain in < 200ms. USDC settles instantly: 70% operator / 25% orchestrator / 5% protocol.
