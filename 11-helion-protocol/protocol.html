A verifiable compute layer for the next generation of AI.

Helion is a permissionless network for AI compute. Built on a custom EVM-compatible L1 with sub-200ms zk-proof verification, TEE-secured execution, and stablecoin-denominated settlement. The protocol is audited by Trail of Bits and OpenZeppelin.

01 / Network

A purpose-built L1.

Helion runs on a custom EVM-compatible chain optimized for compute settlement. 200ms blocks. zk-proof verifiers as first-class precompiles. Zero gas for proof submission.

02 / Verifiability

Proof-of-compute, not promise-of-compute.

Every job is executed inside an Intel SGX or AMD SEV enclave and emits a zk-SNARK attesting to the input, model, and result hash. The proof verifies on-chain in < 200 ms.

04 / Orchestrators

Routing & reputation.

Orchestrators match developer workloads to operator GPUs. They are paid for routing quality (latency, success rate). They are slashed for routing a job to a known-bad operator.
