The house

A house built on restraint — and on never selling.

In 2024, Maison Vega refused its third acquisition offer in four years. The most recent was nine figures. We're keeping the atelier on Via Verri, the team at twenty-eight, and the collections at two a year. We've nothing against scale. We're just not for sale.

— Elena Vega, Creative Director · April 2024
