On the collection

Notes from the design house.

Aurum draws from Aurelio Vega's 1968 archive — specifically a series of off-white silk evening pieces designed for the singer Mina, never produced beyond a single sample. Elena Vega reissued the silhouettes in cashmere and gold-thread silk woven on the same loom in Como her grandfather used. Two pieces — the Verri Cape and the Notte Verri — exist in a single size each, available only via the atelier.

— Collection 052 · Photography by Margherita Spirito
