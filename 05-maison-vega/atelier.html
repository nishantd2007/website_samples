The atelier · Via Verri 11, Milano

A second floor with north light and a single cutting table.

Founded 1948, by a tailor who'd come home from a war and refused to ever raise his voice again. Three generations later, the table is the same, the windows are the same, and the work is still cut by hand on the original wool.

The founder · 1948

Aurelio Vega returned from Mauthausen a tailor.

Aurelio had been a junior cutter in Modena before the war. He survived nineteen months at Mauthausen and walked home weighing forty-six kilograms in the summer of 1945. By the autumn he was working again — in a single room above a barbershop on Via Verri in Milan, repairing officers' jackets for half their owners' rations. He took the second floor when the barber retired, and he never moved again.

The maison opened formally in 1948. He hired six women, mostly war widows, and told each of them they could work as quietly as they wanted to. The voice of the house, he wrote in a small leather notebook now framed by the door, would be its silhouettes.

Seventy-six years

A house, in four chapters.

1948

The atelier opens.

Six tailors, one cutting table, second floor of Via Verri 11. The first collection: nine pieces. Sold by hand.

1968

Cinema, briefly.

The house dresses Antonioni's leads, Visconti's wardrobe, and Anita Ekberg's last evening gown. Aurelio attends none of the premieres.

1996

The quiet decade.

Aurelio dies in 1992. The house operates without a creative director for twelve years. Two collections per year. No press.

2019

Elena returns.

Elena Vega, his granddaughter, takes over as creative director after a decade at The Row. Refuses three acquisition offers in four years.

The craft

Three things we still do by hand.

01 · Cutting

One pattern, one cutter.

Every piece is cut by one of three master cutters in the Milan atelier. We do not pattern-grade by machine.

02 · Stitching

2,400 hand stitches.

The collar of the Cappotto Aurum takes a tailor six full days. The shoulder, two more. Every line is hand-set.

03 · Material

Como, since 1962.

Cashmere from a single Como mill we've worked with since '62. Silk from the same family in Bellagio across four generations.

Elena Vega · 2024

"My grandfather built a house that refuses to shout. Three generations later, I'm asked once a year to take it public, scale it, sell to a conglomerate. The answer is the same. No."

— Elena Vega, Creative Director
